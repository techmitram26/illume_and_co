ILLUME & CO. | Final master and SKU image folders

1. Extract the complete ZIP into one folder.
2. Open Illume_and_Co_Final_Product_Master.xlsx and select sheet 2, Final master.
3. Click an Illume code in column O to open its SKU folder.
4. Columns R and S open the product/source and room use-case images. The wide view is in the same SKU folder wherever the revised brochure provides it.

171 listings and 171 SKU folders. 88 exact-model listings use the attached revised brochure product, close room and wide room views. Existing legacy references remain for the other listings. Image_Source_Index.csv records the source for every listing. All JPEGs are 1200 x 1200 pixels.

Three source differences need confirmation. K2097 and 3001-3 are in the final master but absent from the revised brochure; their folders retain previous references. A888/1 in the final master differs from A888/3 in the brochure. Proposed brochure references are in that SKU folder under Unconfirmed_A888-3.

The green selections and existing dimensions from the attached final sheet are preserved. Older worksheet tabs are included as supplied.
